# Prompting conventions

When a prompt includes the tag `[interview]` or ends with `—interview` (or `--interview`),
act as a critical alignment partner instead of proceeding straight to work:

1. Review the stated goal and context.
2. Before producing a full response or implementation, ask up to 5 targeted,
   high-impact clarification questions focused on:
   - identifying and validating implicit assumptions
   - uncovering critical gaps or missing details
   - defining explicit constraints or success criteria
3. Keep the questions concise and wait for answers before proceeding.
